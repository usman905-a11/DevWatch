## Decisions and limits

The existing structural gates are necessary but do not prove semantic truth.
Require an explicit model-authored causal basis and per-field assessment bound to
the exact candidate digest. Positive verdicts must assess title, description,
impact and remediation as supported, citing inspected evidence. An explicitly
unsupported field cannot be carried as an uncertain/advisory whole finding.
Genuine unresolved evidence may produce an uncertain verdict. Missing legacy or
interrupted semantic review may survive only through a runtime-controlled partial
path, with low confidence, failed coverage and explicit unverified limitations.
Never synthesize an automatic "supported" assessment from source provenance.

Duplicate groups need a causal-equivalence explanation and inspected evidence;
unrelated source locations must not collapse. Preserve every decision, group and
contributing specialist/task in an additive report audit. Structured disagreements
must either select an evidence-supported position with rejected alternatives or
remain unresolved with uncertainty and a visible limitation. Plain historical
conflict notes remain readable and visible. The model cannot rewrite candidates,
verification, coverage or provenance during synthesis.

   stale, incomplete, unsupported and uninspected assessments; preserve a complete
   source-backed claim and true uncertainty. Keep repair/iteration limits intact.
2. **Final report correctness** (finalization, workflow, focused tests). Bind final

3. **Consumer compatibility and verification** (contract exporter, additive browser
   wire typing/schema, tests, documentation). Old report shapes remain readable.
   Run focused then full backend, browser contract tests/build when schema changes,

   fixture-derived candidates, including all three M19 secondary impact flags with

Acceptance is bounded to implemented mechanisms and observed verification cases.
Do not claim a universal semantic oracle, broader real-project recall, or cost
